How a prize wall ticket moves through a Riftbound weekend

How I'm approaching the side event economy

To understand how Riftbound's side-event economy works, I compared the published schedules, payout tables, and prize wall lists from Barcelona, Los Angeles, and Stuttgart. I focused on limited play, specifically draft and sealed, because all three offered formats I could compare side by side. Here's what changed and what I’d watch in Madrid and Philadelphia.

What Stuttgart changed

At Barcelona and Los Angeles, every draft and sealed entrant earned tickets, but no single event earned them enough for a Plated Legend. Stuttgart increased Sealed Deck rewards, added Chaos Draft and two €75 Challenges, and reduced on-demand draft rewards.

My reading

Stuttgart added a way to earn a Plated Legend in a single limited event. The casual changes were more mixed.

  1. The Challenges take time and reward players who keep winning. A perfect record earns exactly 40 tickets, enough for one Plated Legend.
  2. Casual Sealed Deck got more generous! In my model, players earn 48 tickets per 8 instead of 40 at the same €50 entry fee. The new Chaos Draft offers 56, also for €50. On-demand draft drops from 20 to 12, with one of eight players earning nothing.1

Stuttgart's €25 on-demand draft starts every 20 minutes, but has the longest modeled route to 40 tickets: about 27 entries at the average reward. IMO, that raises a question about fun-petitive and com-petitive! How should casual play balance having fun and earning something along the way with a longer-term goal like a Plated Legend?1

What I'd watch in Madrid and Philadelphia

  • Seats filled, event by event.
  • The share of players who leave with no tickets.
  • The share who redeem something at the wall.

Philadelphia opens the week after Madrid. Its formats, fees, and payouts are already set, so what can change that week is on the floor: directions, table assignments, staff briefings, and what players are told about the wall.

What I'm wondering!

  1. How many players wanted a seat and didn't get one?
  2. How does the floor plan shift as the weekend goes on, and do main event tables get repurposed for side events?
  3. How did the actual end times compare with the schedule?
  4. Is prize wall stock allotted per day, or can a hot item sell out on Friday?
  5. Sealed Deck and Chaos Draft were capped at 256 seats and the Challenges at 1,024. How are those caps set, and can tables move between the two tiers once the weekend starts?
  6. Players earn 48 tickets per 8 in casual Sealed Deck and 65 in the Draft Challenge in my model. Which route did more players actually take?
